Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 • Maison Velvet Tote Beam

1. Commitment to Client Confidentiality

Maison Velvet Tote Beam operates with the highest degree of discretion and respect for personal data. This Privacy Policy details the protocols by which we gather, manage, and safeguard information provided through our website, bespoke salon consultations, and private client communications.

2. Information We Collect

When you request a bespoke consultation, order material swatches, or communicate with our concierge, we may collect:

  • Personal contact identifiers (Full Name, Email Address, Telephone Number, Shipping Address).
  • Bespoke commission preferences (monogram initials, customized dimensional requests, leather and velvet choices).
  • Technical telemetry (anonymized browsing patterns, browser version, device resolution) collected via secure Google Analytics.

3. Purpose of Processing

Client information is processed solely to fulfill custom leathercraft orders, organize private salon appointments, manage warranty registries, and provide updates regarding bespoke collections. We never sell, rent, or trade client information to commercial third parties.

4. Data Retention & Rights

You maintain the right to request access to your stored personal records, request corrections, or request complete data deletion by emailing our compliance officer at privacy@velvettotebeam.com.
